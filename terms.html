2. What the app does

Zen Phone Lock helps you focus by restricting distracting apps for a period that you choose. It offers three modes: Zen Focus Lock, Complete Lock, and Zen Balance. You start every session yourself, and every session has an end time shown in the app.

The app uses Android's Accessibility service and Device admin (screen lock only) to do this. You grant these permissions yourself, after a clear disclosure in the app, and you can turn them off at any time in Android Settings.
